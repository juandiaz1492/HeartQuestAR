# ❤️ HeartQuest AR

HeartQuest AR is an educational augmented reality application designed to help healthcare students learn human heart anatomy through interactive 3D exploration and educational minigames.

Developed using **Unity 6, AR Foundation and ARKit**, the application allows users to place a detailed 3D anatomical heart in their real environment, explore its structures and reinforce their knowledge through interactive learning experiences.

The project focuses on making anatomical education more engaging by combining augmented reality, 3D interaction and game-based learning.

## 📅 Week 8 – Initial Minigame Development

During Week 8, we developed three initial minigame prototypes to explore different approaches to learning human heart anatomy.

### 🫀 Explore Heart – AR Exploration

An augmented reality experience that allows users to place and explore a detailed 3D anatomical heart in their real environment.

**Implemented features:**
- AR heart placement on detected horizontal surfaces.
- Locking and unlocking the heart's position.
- Interactive rotation.
- Selection of individual anatomical structures.
- Educational information cards.
- Successful testing on a real iPhone.

## 📅 Week 9 – AR Quiz Development and Application Integration

During Week 9, development focused on expanding AR-based gameplay, improving the user experience and integrating the application's main navigation system.

We also reviewed the existing prototypes to prioritize meaningful AR interactions over full-screen 2D gameplay.

### 🎯 Quiz Challenge – Interactive AR Anatomy Quiz

A new augmented reality minigame was developed, allowing users to answer anatomy questions by selecting structures directly on a 3D heart placed in their physical environment.

**Implemented features:**
- Ten randomized anatomy questions per session.
- Direct selection of anatomical structures on the AR heart.
- Study Mode to lock the heart and begin the quiz.
- Interactive heart rotation.
- One point awarded for correct first-attempt answers.
- Unlimited retries until the correct answer is identified.
- Yellow visual highlighting after three incorrect attempts.
- Manual Hide/Show Ventricular Wall functionality to explore internal heart structures.
- Real-time score and question progression.
- Final score display.
- Successful testing on a physical iPhone.

The minigame emphasizes learning through interaction, allowing users to identify each anatomical structure before continuing.

🎥 **[Watch Quiz Challenge – Week 9 Demo](videos/QuizChallenge_Week9.mp4)**

### 🏠 Main Menu and Navigation

The application's main menu was integrated and adapted for vertical smartphone screens.

**Implemented features:**
- HeartQuest AR main menu.
- Navigation to Explore Heart.
- Navigation to Quiz Challenge.
- Return-to-menu functionality in both AR minigames.
- Updated scene management and iOS build configuration.
- Preservation of existing minigame prototypes for future integration.

The application now provides a connected experience between the main menu and the two functional AR minigames.

🎥 **[Watch Main Menu and Navigation – Week 9 Demo](videos/Navigation_Week9.mp4)**

### Week 9 Summary

By the end of Week 9, HeartQuest AR includes two functional AR learning experiences, an integrated main menu and working navigation between scenes.

The updated application has been tested on a real iPhone, including returning to and re-entering AR minigames successfully.

The project has progressed from individual prototypes towards a unified educational augmented reality application.
